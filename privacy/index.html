Bottle Match privacy policy

Effective 17 November 2026

Bottle Match is a party puzzle played by passing one device around. The Android application id is com.vorithstudio.bottlematch. The iOS bundle id is com.vorithstudio.bottlematch. It is also available in the browser. The app is published by the operator of pasttime.xyz.

Bottle Match is not directed at children under 13. It has no account and no game server of its own.

Information that stays on the device

The app stores this information on the device so a match can continue:

  • Player names you type, and the icon and color chosen for each player
  • The match in progress, including the board, scores, turn, and result
  • Settings for sound, haptics, and reduced motion

The app does not upload player names or the match. Uninstalling the app removes this information from the device. The phone or tabletâ€™s own backup may still include it.

Advertising

The Android and iOS apps show a banner and, after a completed match, one interstitial ad. Ads are requested through Unity LevelPlay (ironSource), which also measures ad quality. The browser build does not request ads.

To choose and measure an ad, LevelPlay and the advertising partners it calls can receive the advertising identifier, IP address, device model, operating system, app version, and whether an ad was shown or tapped. On Android the app declares the Advertising ID permission for this. The app does not show an iOS tracking prompt. The partners currently authorized for this app are listed at pasttime.xyz/app-ads.txt.

Unity describes its own handling at unity.com/legal/privacy-policy.

Firebase Remote Config

The app contacts Google Firebase to read one setting, test_ads, which chooses test or live advertising. Firebase receives an installation identifier and basic app information for that request. The app does not use Firebase Analytics, Authentication, Crashlytics, or a database.

Google describes this handling at firebase.google.com/support/privacy.

What this app does not do

  • It does not ask you to create an account.
  • It does not sell personal information.
  • It does not request your location, contacts, photos, camera, or microphone.
