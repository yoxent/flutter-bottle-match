### Full description

```text
Bottle Match is a party puzzle for one shared phone. Add your names, sit together, and pass the phone. There is no account and no online lobby.

Each round hides an order of bottles. On your turn, rearrange them and submit one arrangement. The app tells you how many bottles are already in the correct place, then hands the phone to the next player. The first exact order wins the round.

Set the table before you play. Choose 2 to 12 players and 5 to 15 bottles. The goal can be the first player to a number of round wins, or one chosen player’s goal. Turn order can be Linear, Snake, Rotating, or Random. A turn timer can be off, or 30, 60, or 90 seconds.

Every round shows the turn order before it begins. A wrong check leaves the board where the last player put it. A correct arrangement scores one point, and nothing else scores. Once everyone has taken three turns, the table can reveal one bottle that is already in the right place. Reach the goal and that player is the champion.

Close the app in the middle of a match and it waits on the phone. Open it again to continue from the same turn.

Bottle Match includes ads.
```
